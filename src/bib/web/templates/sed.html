What am I looking at?

A simple stellar population: all stars born in one instantaneous burst. Every plot shares the age slider and playbar. Click a timeseries plot to jump every panel — including the SED — to that age. Pin an SSP to overlay it on all visible plots, then change IMF, metallicity or binary/single to compare. With one or more pins, sum visible spectra adds the live SSP to every pin at the ages shown (the playhead if sync visible spectra ages is on). That mix is not a star-formation history. Photometry is computed for the current (live) SSP only — pinned overlays are not photometered — using the full-resolution spectrum at the nearest grid age. Redshift shifts only the SED into the observer frame; ionizing photons, star counts and rates stay rest-frame. Trail snapshots drop a faded SED every 0.5 dex while playing, only when nothing is pinned. Star counts use BPASS spectral-type assignments (Eldridge et al. 2017, table 3), not a fit to the plotted SED.

Magnitudes are for the current (live) SSP only — pinned overlays are not photometered. They use that SSP’s full-resolution SED at the nearest grid age (—), not the interpolated display curve. Tick a filter to overlay its passband and the measured magnitude as a point on the SED. The Applied column states whether each magnitude is dust-extinguished, IGM-attenuated, both or unattenuated; an outside dust window or no IGM flag (and a crossed ◇̸ marker) means a requested effect could not be applied inside that passband.

Star counts

Expected number of each type at this age, IMF-weighted, scaled to formed mass.

Ionizing photons

Rest-frame ionizing photon rate. The right axis is the Case B Hα luminosity BPASS tabulates as log L(Hα) = log Nion − 11.87. Independent of the redshift slider.

Supernova rates

Counts in each age bin divided by the bin width. Core-collapse = IIP + II-other + Ib + Ic.

Surviving mass

Mass still in live stars, and in remnants (WD/NS/BH) where BPASS published it.

Chemical yields

Metal mass-loss rate in stellar winds versus supernova ejecta (M☉/yr).
