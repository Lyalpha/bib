If you used v2.2.1 products

This work made use of v2.2.1 of the Binary Population and Spectral Synthesis (BPASS) models as described in Eldridge, Stanway et al. (2017) and Stanway & Eldridge et al. (2018).

If you used v2.3 (alpha-enhanced) products

This work made use of v2.3 of the Binary Population and Spectral Synthesis (BPASS) models as described in Byrne et al. (2022) and Stanway & Eldridge (2018).
